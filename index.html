Health Tips for Students

Nutrition

Eat balanced meals with plenty of fruits, vegetables, and whole grains. Stay hydrated and avoid sugary drinks.

Physical Activity

Aim for at least 60 minutes of physical activity daily. Include both aerobic exercise and strength training.

Sleep Hygiene

Get 8-10 hours of sleep per night. Maintain a consistent sleep schedule and create a relaxing bedtime routine.

Hygiene

Wash hands frequently, especially before eating and after using the bathroom. Keep personal spaces clean.

Mental Wellness

Practice mindfulness, talk about your feelings, and seek help when needed. Take breaks when feeling overwhelmed.

Safety First

Follow safety guidelines, wear appropriate protective gear during activities, and report any unsafe conditions.
